same source-recreation, maintenance, migration, browser and protected-host scope
used for Supplier Onboarding Hub and Major Incident Command.

authentication and Origin checks, complete protected-host lifecycles, exact
source business-state/audit parity and one-use-ticket rejection. Packages were
prepared but not tenant-installed. Live Slack-client operation was unavailable.
