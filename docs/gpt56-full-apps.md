# GPT-5.6 Sol two-full-application result

Two substantial synthetic Slack applications were designed and built:

- **Store Opening Readiness:** programmes, workstreams, readiness tasks,
  blockers, milestones and sign-off.
- **Product Recall Coordination:** recalls, affected lots, store actions,
  acknowledgements, quarantine, escalation, closure and reopen.

Both sources passed deterministic tests. Both final Teams targets preserved the
reviewed domain and contract, passed independent local verification, passed 15
browser scenarios and completed protected-host Teams lifecycles with exact
Slack business-state/audit parity and rejection of a reused one-use ticket.

The result remains **blocked**, not completed. Migration Examples Lab 1.4.0 was
built, but actual tenant upload/installation could not be completed because the
approved minimized Teams renderer was unresponsive and the approved Azure CLI
identity lacked Microsoft Graph AppCatalog scope. No alternate login or
credential inspection was attempted. Product Recall completed its live Slack
lifecycle; Store retained task start/block/unblock/complete/reopen evidence but
did not complete live create and sign-off.
